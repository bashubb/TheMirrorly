How it works

Open. Look. Close. That's the whole app.

1

Open Mirrorly

The front camera comes on instantly, full-screen and mirrored like glass. Brightness jumps to maximum on its own.

2

Tap the bulb if it's dark

The screen border turns bright white and lights your face evenly — no flash, no torch, no shadows.

3

Zoom for the details

Drag the slider or pinch to magnify teeth, brows and contact lenses. Around 2× is the sweet spot.

4

Close it and go

Nothing was photographed, recorded or uploaded. Expect a comment from the mirror on your way out.

FAQ

Questions people ask before installing a mirror app

Is Mirrorly free?

Yes. Mirrorly is free to download from the App Store. There is an optional one-time purchase for lifetime access — no subscription, and no ads either way.

Learn more →

Does iPhone have a built-in mirror app?

No. iOS has no mirror app. People use the Camera app instead, but it's built to take photos — the shutter is under your thumb, brightness isn't maxed, and there's no front light. Mirrorly turns the front camera into a real mirror.

Learn more →

Does Mirrorly save or upload my photos?

No. Mirrorly never captures, stores or uploads photos, video, camera frames or face data. The camera feed and the on-device face analysis stay on your iPhone. There's no account, either.

Learn more →

Does Mirrorly work in the dark?

Yes. Tap the light-bulb button and the edges of the screen turn bright white at maximum brightness, lighting your face like a small ring light — useful in a car at night, a dim bathroom or a hotel room.

Learn more →

Can I zoom in to check my teeth or eyebrows?

Yes. Drag the zoom slider or pinch the screen to magnify. Around 2× is enough to spot food between teeth, seat a contact lens or tweeze a stray hair.

Learn more →

Is this the same as screen mirroring?

No. Screen mirroring (AirPlay) sends your iPhone's display to a TV. Mirrorly is a mirror — it shows your face using the front camera, the way glass does.

Learn more →

Why do I look different in Mirrorly than in my selfies?

Mirrorly always shows you mirrored, like real glass — the version of your face you're used to. Selfies are saved un-mirrored, the way other people see you, which is why photos can look subtly "off".

Learn more →

Why is the mirror talking to me?

That's Mirrorly's personality. On-device face analysis reads your expression and replies with a dry one-liner like "okay, smug." or "step back, detective." Nothing about your face leaves the phone.

Learn more →

Does Mirrorly work on Android?

No. Mirrorly is an iOS app for iPhone, available on the App Store.
