Questions

Does Mirrorly save or upload anything?
No. There is no shutter. It never captures, stores or uploads photos, video, camera frames or face data, and the expression reading runs on your iPhone. No account, no ads.
Can I turn the comments off?
Yes. Switch Mirror Reactions off in Settings and it stays a quiet, very good mirror.
How much will it cost?
Your first three mirror checks are free. After that, one purchase unlocks it for good. No subscription.
Which iPhones will it support?
iPhones running iOS 26 or later.

It also talks back. A little.

Once in a while Mirrorly reads your face and says something to you. Make a face and find out what.
