Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy covers Mirrorly, which is operated by Hubert Kiełkowski, together with the related website or services described here. It covers the app on iOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Purchase or subscription information used to unlock or manage paid features

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Identifiers needed to recognize an account, device, session, or subscription state

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

Some of this data may be linked to a person, account, or persistent identifier.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: RevenueCat and Inc. Used for purchase and subscription analytics, transaction history, entitlement management, and revenue reporting.
  • Backend: RevenueCat and Inc. Used for purchase validation, entitlement management, subscription status, fraud prevention, and purchase restoration.
  • Email / Messaging / Support: Google LLC (Gmail). Used for customer support and privacy rights requests.
  • Payments: Apple Inc. (App Store and StoreKit) and RevenueCat and Inc. Used for processing and validating in-app purchases, managing entitlements, and restoring purchases.
  • Website Hosting: GitHub and Inc. (GitHub Pages). Used for hosting and delivering the app website and privacy policy.

Additional feature details:

  • StoreKit: Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase or subscription information to validate transactions, manage entitlements, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and provide purchase analytics. This data is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data may be shared with outside recipients such as RevenueCat and Inc. and Apple Inc. (App Store and StoreKit) when that is needed to operate the app’s features.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: RevenueCat and Inc. Used for purchase and subscription analytics, transaction history, entitlement management, and revenue reporting. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Backend: RevenueCat and Inc. Used for purchase validation, entitlement management, subscription status, fraud prevention, and purchase restoration. May receive personal data needed for this role. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Email / Messaging / Support: Google LLC (Gmail). Used for customer support and privacy rights requests. May receive personal data needed for this role. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Payments: Apple Inc. (App Store and StoreKit) and RevenueCat and Inc. Used for processing and validating in-app purchases, managing entitlements, and restoring purchases. May receive personal data needed for this role. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Website Hosting: GitHub and Inc. (GitHub Pages). Used for hosting and delivering the app website and privacy policy. May receive personal data needed for this role. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Purchase and subscription information and an anonymous app user identifier are shared with RevenueCat to validate purchases, manage entitlements, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and provide subscription analytics. Apple processes payments through the App Store. Camera images, video, and face-analysis data are never shared and remain on the device.

Payments and Subscriptions

The app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions through StoreKit.

Payment-related providers include Apple Inc. (App Store and StoreKit) and RevenueCat and Inc. They are used for processing and validating in-app purchases, managing entitlements, and restoring purchases. These providers may receive the purchase, billing, account, or receipt data needed to process payments and manage entitlements.

Purchase or subscription information may be used to manage billing, entitlements, support, refunds, or account access.

Social Logins and Public Features

The app or related services include Sharing to social media. Information people post or share through these features may be visible to other users or to the public, depending on how the feature works.

If a user shares content to a third-party social platform, that platform’s privacy policy also applies to the shared content.

The app provides Apple’s system share sheet so users may voluntarily share the App Store link and a short message through a destination they select. The app does not upload or share camera images, videos, face-analysis data, or other user content.

Data Retention

Retention varies by feature. Camera and face-analysis data are not retained. Local preferences remain until the app is deleted or reset. Purchase, support, and website data are retained only as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes.
