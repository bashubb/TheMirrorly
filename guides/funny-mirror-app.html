The Funny Mirror App That Judges You (Lovingly)

Checking your face is a five-second chore you do a dozen times a day. Mirrorly makes it a five-second bit: it watches your expression and drops a dry one-liner. It's still a proper mirror — the jokes are just the reason people keep opening it.

What it actually says

Mirrorly reads your expression using on-device analysis and picks a caption to match — there are over four hundred of them. Straighten your tie with a serious face and you'll get "easy there, inspector." Grin at yourself and it's "okay, smug little smile." Lean in to inspect your pores: "step back, pore detective." Open it in a dark room: "who approved this lighting." Come back for the fourth time: "one more check. naturally."

The tone is deadpan rather than slapstick — a mirror that's seen it all and is mildly unimpressed, in a way that makes you laugh at the fact that you're checking your hair again.

Still a real mirror first

The captions sit on top of a mirror that takes the job seriously: full-screen front-camera view, maximum brightness the moment it opens, a ring light for dark rooms, and zoom for teeth, brows and lenses. The humour is the personality, not the product — and if you want a quiet moment with your reflection, Mirror Reactions switches off in settings.

About the face analysis

Because "an app that reads my face" is a reasonable thing to be wary of: the analysis runs entirely on your iPhone. Nothing about your face is stored or sent anywhere — no frames, no photos, no face data. There's no account, and no ads. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Show it to someone

It's a good bit at a table of friends — hand the phone around and let the mirror judge everyone in turn. There's a share button that sends the App Store link so they can get judged on their own phone.

How to do it in Mirrorly

To get judged by your own mirror:

  1. Install Mirrorly and open it. The mirror is on immediately.
  2. Make a face. Any face. Read the caption at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Try the light-bulb in a dark room and the zoom slider up close — the mirror has something to say about both.
  4. Use Share with friends in settings to send the App Store link to whoever needs judging next.
